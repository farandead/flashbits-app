## Usage Tips

1. **Keep it short** - Screenshot text should be 3-7 words max
2. **Use numbers** - "2,000+ questions" is more compelling than "many questions"
3. **Focus on benefits** - What's in it for the user?
4. **Use action words** - Swipe, Level Up, Master, Ace
5. **Highlight uniqueness** - Emphasize the swipe-based, gamified approach
6. **Appeal to emotions** - Excitement, confidence, achievement
