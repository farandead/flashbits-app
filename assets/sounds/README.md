# Sound Assets (Optional)

**The app works without these files!** Sounds are optional - the app will function normally without them.

To enable sound effects, place your sound files here:

- `correct.mp3` - Sound to play when answer is correct
- `incorrect.mp3` - Sound to play when answer is incorrect

## Recommended Sound Resources

You can find free sound effects from:
- https://freesound.org/
- https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/
- https://www.zapsplat.com/

## Specifications

- Format: MP3
- Duration: 0.5 - 2 seconds (short and snappy)
- File size: Keep under 100KB for each

## Example Sounds

**Correct Answer:**
- Success chime
- Positive beep
- Achievement sound
- "Ding" sound

**Incorrect Answer:**
- Buzzer sound
- Error beep
- Negative tone
- "Wrong" sound
