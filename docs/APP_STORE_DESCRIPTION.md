## Description (4,000 characters max)

Master coding interviews the fun way. flashbits transforms interview prep into an addictive, swipe-based experience. Practice thousands of real interview questions from top tech companies, earn XP, build streaks, and level up your coding skills—all in a sleek, gamified interface.

Gamified Learning Experience

Swipe through questions like your favorite social feed. Earn XP and unlock hacker ranks as you progress from n00b to Legend. Build daily streaks and hit milestone rewards that keep you motivated. Track your progress with detailed stats and analytics that show exactly how you're improving over time.

Massive Question Bank

Access over 2,000 coding interview questions covering algorithms, data structures, system design, and more. Practice with questions from Google, Meta, Amazon, Apple, Microsoft, and other top tech companies. Questions are organized by difficulty levels from Easy and Medium to Hard and Cracked, so you can challenge yourself at the right level.

Smart Practice Features

Filter questions by topic, difficulty, or company to focus on exactly what you need to practice. Download questions for offline practice and study anywhere, even without an internet connection. Get instant feedback with detailed explanations that help you understand not just what the answer is, but why it's correct. Track which questions you've answered correctly, incorrectly, or skipped so you can review your weak areas.

Built for Speed and Efficiency

The swipe-based interface eliminates scrolling and clicking, letting you move through questions quickly and naturally. Get instant answer feedback so you can learn from mistakes immediately. Smooth animations and haptic feedback make the experience feel polished and responsive. Dark mode is optimized for late-night study sessions, reducing eye strain while you practice.

Comprehensive Progress Tracking

See your accuracy, streak, and XP at a glance on the main screen. Monitor your performance by topic and difficulty to identify areas that need more work. Review previously answered questions to reinforce learning. Watch your hacker rank climb as you improve, providing a clear visual representation of your progress.

Your Data, Your Control

Secure authentication options include email, Apple Sign In, or GitHub, giving you flexibility in how you access your account. All progress is synced across devices, so you can practice on your phone, tablet, or any device. Questions are saved locally for offline access, ensuring you can study even when you don't have internet connectivity.

Perfect for Software Engineers

flashbits is designed for software engineers preparing for interviews, computer science students building their skills, career changers entering tech, and developers looking to level up their abilities. Whether you're targeting FAANG companies or just want to improve your coding interview skills, flashbits provides the practice you need.

Why flashbits Works

Traditional interview prep is boring. flashbits makes it engaging, addictive, and effective. The swipe-based format keeps you moving through questions quickly, the gamification system provides motivation through XP and ranks, and the comprehensive question bank ensures you're practicing real interview scenarios. Swipe through questions, earn rewards, and build the skills you need to land your dream job.

Download flashbits today and start your journey from n00b to Legend.
